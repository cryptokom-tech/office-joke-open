## The joke

> Someone labelled their lunch 'DO NOT EAT — Dave'. Dave left in 2021. The lunch is now our longest-serving employee.

115 characters, 117 bytes. A shared-fridge label, a colleague who left years
ago, and a lunch that outlasted him — the turn pays off in the last few words.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only, no network, deterministic) writes the joke
to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` and self-checks the 1–200 character gate
before exiting. See [CANDIDATES.md](CANDIDATES.md) for the 16 original
candidates and the local pairwise judging against the judge brief.
